Academic Essay Title: (Choose 1 of the 6 titles) 
 
Describe how globalisation has influenced the meaning and use of English worldwide. 
 
 
 
 
Introduction 
Hook Statement 
(1 Sentence) 
English has become a common language used by more than a billion 
people all over the world due to the fact that globalization has not only 
expanded its influence but also changed its significance and function in 
the 21st century. 
Background 
Information 
(to show context & 
importance similar to 
the title) 
(At least 3 Bullet 
Points) 
 
 
• English was once spread by colonization, but with globalization, 
it has been more widely used in new fields such as science, 
academics and international organizations. 
• Globalization is characterized by the rapid cross-border exchange 
of politics, culture, society and information, so people from 
different countries need a common way of communication. 
• The rise of digital platforms and social media provides a new 
space for language innovation, which is no longer bound by 
traditional language rules. 
• English is widely used as a medium for education (EMI), 
academic publishing and business management, which has 
resulted in linguistic and cultural prejudice that may ignore other 
languages. 
Thesis Statement 
(1 Sentence) 
Globalization has made English an indisputable world language, but this 
process is full of contradictions : the informal diversification of English 
is realised through digital application, but the subtle neo-imperialist 
form of language leads to the marginalization of the local language.

semantics and usage of English more diverse
