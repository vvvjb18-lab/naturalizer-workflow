A fascinating finding is that information opposing genetically modified foods keeps emerging. When the controversy of GM foods emerges, countless organizations fighting for environmental and consumer rights have stepped up their opposition to GM foods. Additionally, foods made from or utilizing GM organisms (GMOs), as defined by the World Health Organization (2014), are known as GM foods or transgenic foods. Consumers' worries about the safety of GM foods develop owing to the influx of misleading information from social media and websites. Due to safety concerns, mandatory labeling for GM foods is implemented in many countries, however, it is debatable whether they will be effective in altering consumer perceptions. This essay will examine the reasons for the emergence of GM foods, a modern healthy lifestyle and rising food demand, and the evident beneficial and negative effects.

Firstly, the production of GM foods is mostly driven by the present trend toward healthier lifestyles and a rise in food demand. According to Kubisz et al. (2021), GM foods are enhanced with nutrients that have a high level of pro-health value, such as vitamin E and unsaturated fatty acids, and offer consumers an alternative to non-GM foods. Since GM foods allow for better nutrient absorption than non-GM foods including probiotics, many people opt to choose them in order to prevent serious illnesses and maintain their health. Besides, in research by Gerland et al. (2014), the world population will reach 9.8 billion by 2050. As the imbalance between crop yields and population growth rate, the food supply will be inadequate in the foreseeable future. Bailey-Serres et al. (2019) state that GM foods could be the solution to insufficient food supply since it provides stable yields to meet the growing food demand. Accordingly, GM foods play a crucial role in fulfilling the needs of people all over the world.

On the plus side, GM foods have generated products with both medical and economic benefits. In terms of medical use, GM foods can act as oral vaccines, including transgenic maize, that stimulate the immune system to create antibodies (Zhang et al. 2016). They can protect individuals against a variety of illnesses, inclusive of infectious ones, and raise immunization levels. A notable GM food example is Golden Rice, which was developed as a treatment for vitamin A deficiency to give the underprivileged in developing countries more vitamin A. Therefore, it can reduce the risk of malnutrition in those living in poverty. Regarding economic advantages, Brookes (2022) discovered that between 1996 and 2020, farmers that adopted GM technology had an increase in farm revenue of $261.3 billion US dollars. Because of GM crops’ lower production costs, farmers will be able to get a bigger monetary return and generate more foods at reasonable rates enabling purchasing products at a lower price by consumers, especially the impoverished. Hence, GM foods are favorable to long-term development.

Highlighting the negative consequences of GM foods, the danger of food allergies, and the disruption of ecosystem complexity. Vega Rodríguez et al. (2022) note that food allergy is linked to varieties that have genes with an allergizing potential. For example, genetically modified soybean that has methionine from a Brazil nut injected for nutritional purposes runs the likelihood of allergic responses in humans, especially those having Brazil nut allergy. The consumption of GM foods can induce sequentially skin reactions and alterations in the respiratory system. Concerning the environment, Tsatsakis et al.(2017) state that the reduction of weed diversity is attributed to herbicide-resistant crops, which disrupt the habitats of beneficial farmland organisms and ultimately complex food webs. In addition, farmers are forced to apply more herbicides due to the emergence of superweeds that are resistant to glyphosate because if not Fusarium could produce toxins that could penetrate the animal food chain, explained by Ardekani & Shirzad (2019). Overall, GM foods constitute a threat to human health and the environment.

This essay has outlined the causes of the appearance of GM foods along with the major impacts. The development of GM foods stems from megatrends namely a healthy lifestyle and an increase in food demand, which is vital for further human advancement. Meanwhile, GM foods have health and financial advantages that farmers and those in need can benefit from. However, GM foods are responsible for food allergies and disruption of the environment which provide the basis for criticism from GM technology opponents. In conclusion, numerous long-term trials for the safety of GM technology will keep examined in the future to gain consumers’ acceptance of GM foods.
