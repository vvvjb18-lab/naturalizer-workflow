According to Feuerbach, (1848), we are what we eat. Food is essential in the lives of human beings which is why people get concerned about what they eat. For the longest time, human beings have been using traditional methods in producing food. However, innovations such as mutagenesis and hybridization came with many improvements from the crossbred grains. In the last decades, genetic engineering development gradually changed the world food production industry with the production of Genetically Modified (GM) foods. According to Dadgarnejad et al., (2017), World Health Organization describes GM food as food obtained from organisms that have been genetically modified. Avery et al., (1995) discovered that DNA is transferrable between organisms, and the first GM food crop was produced using an antibiotic-resistant plant. Although there is increasing use of GM foods, a lot of people are yet to familiarize themselves with GM foods. This essay, therefore, this essay describes the causes of GM food demand and how it affects the environment and public health.

	One cause for GM foods is to satisfy the increased demand for foods that traditional food production methods couldn’t. According to Zhang et al., (2016), GM foods are needed because the current world population is increasing day by day. Undernourishment can result and so to end hunger, especially in developing regions, GM crops should be planted in large. The authors add that decrease in arable land is another reason and so to solve this, an alternative means is to grow GM crops to produce high yields. Dadgarnejad et al., (2017), add that there is an increased population globally, and to satisfy food demands, food production should as well be increased. For instance, China with its dense population and arable land has already invested in GM crops to meet its people’s food demands. 

	One effect of GM foods is that they could be hazardous to the ecosystem and damage biodiversity badly. Tsatsakis et al., (2017), explain that GM feed and food affects not only human health but also disturbs biodiversity and ecosystem creations. The authors add that the ecosystem is affected adversely by the use of herbicides, insecticides, and weed resistance on GM crops. Disturbing the natural habitats leads to biodiversity getting affected. According to Dadgarnejad et al., (2017), GM technology is a threat to biodiversity and the evolution of resistant pests. The authors add that the ecosystem is badly affected and that the soil, water, animals, and air risk getting affected by the GM release into the environment. in addition, beneficial insects may be harmed and the food web weakened because of the pesticides in the GM crops (Zhu et al., 2016). 

	A positive effect of GM foods is that they have nutritional value and improve people’s health. Kamthan et al., (2016) explain that GM foods are nutritious and can help fight malnutrition because it normally has high yields. GM food crops can as well tolerate abiotic and biotic stresses. The authors add that GM food crops are resistant to different gene organisms making them safe for consumption. The authors also add that GM foods are of high quality and can meet high world demand. GM foods are quality and the desire to eat healthy diets drives consumers to purchase the perceived quality GM foods (Hakim et al., 2020). According to Farid et al., (2020), GM foods are full of nutritional value since the food engineering techniques used by scientists ensure that farmers cultivate and produce quality foods. People nowadays are more concerned about their health and will likely purchase these GM foods because of the quality guaranteed. 

	The essay explained the causes and effects of GM foods. The cause identified is that GM foods satisfy the increased demand for food. The negative effect is that GM foods affect both the ecosystem and biodiversity badly while the positive effect is that GM foods have nutritional value and improve people’s health. To sum up, GM foods are a key solution to fighting hunger and should be encouraged globally since they are nutritious. More studies on the harmful effects of GM crops should however be done and possible solutions offered to protect the ecosystem and biodiversity.
